Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a lawyer before starting a legal case?

While it is possible to handle certain legal matters on your own, engaging a qualified lawyer from the outset can help you understand your rights, avoid costly mistakes, and ensure the correct legal procedures are followed. Seeking legal advice early often improves your chances of resolving a matter efficiently and effectively.

What types of legal services do you provide?

The Chambers of I.J. Obijuru-Uzodinma, ESQ provides legal services across a broad range of practice areas, including civil litigation, corporate & commercial law, property transactions, contract drafting & review, family law, employment matters, legal advisory services, and dispute resolution. If you’re unsure whether your matter falls within our practice, please contact us for guidance.

How do I schedule a consultation?

Scheduling a consultation is simple. You may contact our office by phone, email, or through the contact form on our website. During your consultation, we’ll listen to your concerns, assess your legal matter, and discuss the most appropriate course of action based on your circumstances.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

For your initial consultation, it’s helpful to bring any documents relevant to your legal matter, including contracts, correspondence, court documents, identification, or any other records that may assist us in understanding your case. The more information you provide, the better we can evaluate your situation.

How much do your legal services cost?

Legal fees vary depending on the nature, complexity, and scope of the services required. Following an initial consultation, we’ll explain the expected costs and provide a clear fee arrangement before any legal work begins, ensuring transparency throughout the process.

Can legal disputes be resolved without going to court?

Yes. Many legal disputes can be resolved through negotiation, mediation, arbitration, or other forms of alternative dispute resolution. Where appropriate, we work toward practical and cost-effective solutions outside the courtroom while protecting our clients’ rights and interests.

How long will it take to resolve my legal matter?

The timeline for resolving a legal matter can vary significantly based on its complexity and the court's schedule. Simple cases might be resolved within a few months, while more complex matters could take several years. During your initial consultation, we can provide a more accurate estimate based on the specifics of your case.
